Our story

Fused by hand.
Built by students.

We are a team of high schoolers in the Junior Achievement Company Program. We design every sprite, sort every color, and fuse every piece ourselves — usually with a podcast on and a lot of snacks nearby.

BitByBit is a real company: we handle product design, pricing, sales days, and the messy parts in between. The pixels are just the fun part.
