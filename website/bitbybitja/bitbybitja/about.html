Who we are

Students, beads, and a little bit of magic.

BitByBit is a student-run company in the Junior Achievement Company Program. We turn tiny plastic beads into pixel art — and turn a school project into a real little business.

Student-run Handmade JA Company Program

Our story

We started with one pegboard and a big idea.

BitByBit began as a team of high schoolers who wanted to make something real. We loved retro games and pixel art, and we loved the idea that a handful of tiny beads could become something people keep on their desks or keys.

Through the Junior Achievement Company Program, we built a company from scratch — product design, pricing, sales days, and all the spreadsheets in between. Every piece is designed, sorted, fused, and packed by students.

  • Every product is made by hand, in small batches, by our team.
  • We sell in person at JA sales days and arrange pickup for custom orders.
  • A share of what we earn goes to a charity chosen by the team.

The process

From pegboard to product.

No factory, no 3D printer. Just a grid, a pair of tweezers, and an iron.

01

Pick a pixel

We sketch a sprite on a pixel grid, then choose a bead palette that will still read clearly at keychain size.

02

Sort the beads

Each color comes from its own bin. Tiny beads, big patience. This is also when someone inevitably drops a tray.

03

Fuse it

We place every bead by hand and iron the piece until the edges melt together, then weight it flat while it cools.

04

Finish + pack

Hardware, frames, and felt backs go on next. Every piece gets a final check before it goes in the sale bin.

The honest bit: fused bead art is sturdy, but it is not indestructible. Keep pieces out of hot cars, avoid soaking them, and they will stick around for a long time.

What we stand for

Small pieces. Strong values.

Make it by hand

Every piece is designed and assembled by a student. If it has a slightly different bead count than the one next to it, that is the point.

Keep it playful

We make things that make us smile first. If a pixel ghost or a tiny duck earns a spot in the lineup, it earned it.

Give it back

A share of our proceeds goes to a charity our team chooses together. The full pledge will be announced on our charity page.

The program

Built inside Junior Achievement.

The JA Company Program gives high schoolers the chance to build and run a real company with support from teachers and volunteer advisors. We choose a product, handle the money, plan sales, and decide what to do with the results.

For us, that means late-night bead sorting, trial-and-error pricing, and learning that a company is a lot more than the thing it sells. BitByBit is the product of that work.
