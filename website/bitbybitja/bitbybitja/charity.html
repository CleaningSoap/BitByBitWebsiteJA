Our chosen charity

Upstream Ottawa Mental Health Community Support.

Since 1985, Upstream Ottawa has provided frontline community mental health services in Ottawa. The organization supports youth, adults, and families living with mental health and substance-use health challenges.

Their work includes case management, wellness programs, social and recreational outings, practical supports, and help with everyday essentials. We chose Upstream because their support is local, practical, and reaches people in our community.

Why this charity

Local support, close to home.

Upstream Ottawa has been part of the city's community mental health landscape since 1985. What started as a grassroots response to gaps in local services has grown into comprehensive care for youth, adults, and families.

We like that the money stays local and supports practical, everyday help — from case management to meals, transportation, and social connection. That is the kind of impact we want our magnets to have.

  • Community-based mental health support in Ottawa since 1985.
  • Serves youth, adults, and families across the city.
  • Registered Canadian charity.
