How a sales day works.

No appointment, no online cart. Just a table, a bunch of bead art, and the team who made it.

01

Check the calendar

Dates and times live here. We add locations as soon as they are confirmed — look for the event type and location on each sale.

02

Find the table

Look for the perler bead display and the BitByBit sign. Say hi, ask how the pieces are made, and inspect the pixels up close.

03

Take home a pixel

Pick your favorite piece while supplies last. Payment options are confirmed per event — ask us if you are unsure.
