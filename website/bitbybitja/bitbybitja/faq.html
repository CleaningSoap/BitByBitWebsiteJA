Products & care

How are the pieces made?

Everything starts as a pixel grid. We place perler beads on a pegboard, fuse them with heat, then add hardware, frames, or felt backs. Browse the about page for the full process.

Are the pieces durable?

Fused bead art is sturdy enough for keys, bags, and desks, but it is handmade and can break if dropped on a hard floor. Keep pieces out of hot cars and away from direct heat, and avoid soaking them in water.

How do I clean a piece?

Wipe it with a slightly damp cloth and dry it right away. Do not put bead art in the dishwasher, washing machine, or microwave. For framed pieces, dust gently instead of using water.

Is bead art safe for young kids?

Small beads and finished pieces can be a choking hazard. These products are not intended for young children, and an adult should supervise older kids. Keep loose beads and small magnets away from pets and children.

Will my piece look exactly like the photo?

Every piece is made by hand, so small differences in bead placement and color are normal. Screens also display colors differently. If a specific color matters to you, ask us before a sales day.
